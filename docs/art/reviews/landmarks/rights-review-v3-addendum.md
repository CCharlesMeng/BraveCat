# Landmark scenes rights and provenance review v3 addendum

Review date and source access date: **2026-07-20**

Scope: **5 new destinations / 13 active generated scenes**

Decision: **not cleared for shipping**

> This is a conservative operational art-and-rights review, not legal advice. It identifies evidence, uncertainties, and release gates; qualified counsel and the relevant authorities should decide disputed questions of copyright, adaptation, monument reproduction, cultural-property control, trademark, traditional cultural expression, and territorial scope.

## Executive summary

This addendum reviews only the five destinations and 13 active scenes added by the v3 expansion. The 20 earlier destinations and 48 scenes remain governed by [the v1 rights review](rights-review-v1.md) and are not reassessed here. The v3 scenes are visually approved, but rights approval remains a shipping prerequisite. ([v3 expansion plan](../../landmark-expansion-v3.json); [v3 candidate manifest](../../candidates/landmarks/manifest.candidates.v3.json); [v3 review index](../../candidates/landmarks/review-index.v3.md); accessed 2026-07-20)

No v3 scene is cleared today because all 13 inherit v1's unresolved common gate for repository style-reference rights, generator records, human contribution, output similarity, trademark review, and release controls. Cursor requires the user to hold the necessary input rights and assigns only Anysphere's rights, if any, in outputs; its terms do not clear third-party rights. ([v1 common gates](rights-review-v1.md#common-gates-for-all-48-scenes); [Cursor Terms, §§1.2 and 5.3](https://cursor.com/terms-of-service); [v3 candidate manifest](../../candidates/landmarks/manifest.candidates.v3.json); accessed 2026-07-20)

Destination-specific result, subject to that common gate:

- **LOW — 1 destination / 2 scenes:** Petra / Al-Khazneh has a plausible clearing path after source-independence, similarity, modern-element, and brand checks; no destination-specific permission rule for the present remotely generated ancient-façade scenes was identified in the reviewed first-party sources. ([official Petra FAQ](https://www.visitpetra.jo/en/faqs); [PDTRA copyright policy](https://pdtra.gov.jo/En/Pages/Copyrights); accessed 2026-07-20)
- **MEDIUM — 1 destination / 3 scenes:** Piopiotahi / Milford Sound has a plausible clearing path, but naming, kea, and cultural-story use need documented mana-whenua-informed review, while DOC and Ngāi Tahu source-content boundaries must remain intact. ([Ngāi Tahu Piopiotahi account](https://ngaitahu.iwi.nz/opportunities-and-resources/publications/te-karaka/reimagining-piopiotahi/); [DOC copyright policy](https://www.doc.govt.nz/footer-links/copyright/); accessed 2026-07-20)
- **HIGH — 1 destination / 2 scenes:** Aït Ben Haddou must be held for Moroccan counsel because the ksar is the main subject of a commercial depiction, Morocco's public-place exception is limited in that circumstance, and the application of architecture, restoration, and folklore provisions is unresolved. ([Moroccan Law No. 2-00, arts. 3, 7 and 20](https://mjcc.gov.ma/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/loi-droits-dauteurs-2014-1.pdf); [UNESCO property record](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/444); accessed 2026-07-20)

## Method and risk labels

The `low`, `medium`, `high`, and `blocked` labels retain the operational meanings defined in v1 and are product recommendations, not findings that a use is lawful or unlawful. This addendum separately evaluates source-photo/reference obligations, generated-output adaptation uncertainty, monument/cultural-property/site-policy rights, trademark/brand concerns, and in-game versus marketing use. ([v1 method and labels](rights-review-v1.md#method-and-risk-labels); accessed 2026-07-20)

Facts and ideas are distinct from protected expression, but “no copied pixels” does not by itself decide whether an output is an adaptation; that question depends on the applicable law and the protected expression actually used. No comprehensive national or international trademark search was performed. ([Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/); [WIPO trademark overview](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/trademarks); accessed 2026-07-20)

## Conservative common provenance gate inherited from v1

The complete v1 common gate applies without relaxation to all 13 scenes. The v3 records identify the same three repository style-reference paths and describe style-only use, but they do not establish author, acquisition source, license, or permission for those images; model and seed are also unavailable. ([v1 common gates](rights-review-v1.md#common-gates-for-all-48-scenes); [v3 candidate manifest](../../candidates/landmarks/manifest.candidates.v3.json); accessed 2026-07-20)

Before any in-game, storefront, trailer, advertisement, social, press-kit, or merchandise use:

1. **Input rights:** obtain a signed provenance declaration and ownership/license/acquisition evidence for every repository style reference; archive the exact files and hashes. Cursor's terms require all rights, licenses, and permissions needed for submitted inputs. ([Cursor Terms, §1.2](https://cursor.com/terms-of-service); [v1 input gate](rights-review-v1.md#1-input-and-generation-provenance); accessed 2026-07-20)
2. **Generator record:** preserve dated Cursor terms, account/plan, generation timestamps, complete prompts, rejected attempts, normalization records, hashes, and any provider/model information Cursor can supply. Cursor assigns only Anysphere's rights, if any, and disclaims non-infringement. ([Cursor Terms, §§5.3 and 14](https://cursor.com/terms-of-service); accessed 2026-07-20)
3. **Human contribution:** record who selected, cropped, normalized, edited, arranged, and approved each output. The U.S. Copyright Office treats sufficient human authorship case by case and separately from infringement. ([U.S. Copyright Office AI Report, Part 2](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf); accessed 2026-07-20)
4. **Source and similarity review:** archive each source page and its terms; confirm that no photograph, gallery image, map, article figure, screenshot, or source pixel entered generation; and run reverse-image plus human side-by-side review against all cited pages and common commercial photographs for every final PNG. ([Creative Commons adaptation FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#when-is-my-use-considered-an-adaptation); [UNESCO image policy](https://whc.unesco.org/en/disclaimer/); accessed 2026-07-20)
5. **License ledger:** if UNESCO CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO description text, DOC CC BY 4.0 material, or other licensed expression is reproduced rather than merely consulted for facts, add the required credit, license link, change indication, and any ShareAlike treatment counsel determines applies. UNESCO separately restricts photographs and other website elements. ([UNESCO image policy](https://whc.unesco.org/en/disclaimer/); [DOC copyright policy](https://www.doc.govt.nz/footer-links/copyright/); [Creative Commons attribution FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#how-do-i-properly-attribute-material-offered-under-a-creative-commons-license); accessed 2026-07-20)
6. **Marks and contexts:** conduct territory- and class-specific searches; use place names descriptively; remove authority, government, UNESCO/World Heritage, tourism, and operator marks; and avoid “official,” “licensed,” sponsorship, or endorsement language without written support. ([WIPO trademark overview](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/trademarks); [UNESCO emblem/linking policy](https://whc.unesco.org/en/disclaimer/); accessed 2026-07-20)
7. **Release control:** keep `shippingEligible=false` until the common and destination-specific gates close separately for the game and each promotional channel. ([v3 review index](../../candidates/landmarks/review-index.v3.md); [v1 release controls](rights-review-v1.md#4-release-controls); accessed 2026-07-20)

([IMPI MARCia](https://marcia.impi.gob.mx/marcas/search/quick); [WIPO trademark overview](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/trademarks); accessed 2026-07-20)

([Federal Monuments Law, art. 17](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LFMZAAH.pdf); [implementing regulation, arts. 39–41](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/regley/Reg_LFMZAAH_031220.pdf); accessed 2026-07-20)
