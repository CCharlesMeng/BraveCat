# Table Mountain, Cape Town — visual-reference research

Checked 2026-07-20. This note is limited to geometry and spatial relationships: the broad summit, the placement of Devil's Peak and Lion's Head, Cape Town below/in front of the massif, and the orographic “tablecloth.”

## Rights and handling

- **No source photograph or source-image file was copied, downloaded, embedded, or retained.** This file contains links and geometry notes only.
- “Page accessed” below means only that the factual source page was publicly reachable during research. It does **not** grant rights in an image displayed on that page.

- **Restricted photo retained: No** for every source below.

## Geometry established by the sources

- South African Tourism describes Table Mountain's main form as a level plateau approximately 3 km across, edged by cliffs; it says the plateau is flanked by Devil's Peak and Lion's Head and that the larger group forms the amphitheatre around the City Bowl and Table Bay Harbour: <https://www.southafrica.net/uk/en/travel/article/explore-the-rugged-intensely-beautiful-mountain-ranges-of-south-africa>.
- The City of Cape Town fixes the compass relationship: Table Valley is at the foot of Table Mountain, with Devil's Peak to the east and Lion's Head to the west: <https://resource.capetown.gov.za/documentcentre/Documents/Procedures,%20guidelines%20and%20regulations/Company's%20Garden%20Self%20Guided%20Walk.pdf>.
- SANParks supplies the viewer-relative check: when Table Mountain is faced head-on, Lion's Head is the peak on the right: <https://www.sanparks.org/parks/table-mountain/what-to-do/attractions/lions-head-signal-hill>.
- A photographed Signal Hill panorama labels the frontal left-to-right order as Devil's Peak, Table Mountain, then Lion's Head: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Devil%27s_Peak,_Table_Mountain_and_Lion%27s_Head_panorama_from_Signal_Hill.jpg>.
- South African Tourism identifies the “table-cloth” as orographic cloud forming on the plateau: <https://www.southafrica.net/uk/en/travel/article/explore-the-rugged-intensely-beautiful-mountain-ranges-of-south-africa>. A dedicated photograph's author describes a thin cloud layer crossing the summit, descending the camera-facing slope, and evaporating: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cape_Town,_Table_Mountain,_Table_Cloth.jpg>.

- **Page accessed / factual use:** Publicly reachable. It is the strongest official single-page factual anchor for the approximately 3 km level plateau, cliff edges, flanking peaks, City Bowl/Table Bay Harbour amphitheatre, and the orographic tablecloth: <https://www.southafrica.net/uk/en/travel/article/explore-the-rugged-intensely-beautiful-mountain-ranges-of-south-africa>.
- **Geometry-only facts supported:** Draw Table Mountain as a long, nearly horizontal central block rather than a pointed peak. Place Devil's Peak and Lion's Head as lateral flanks; place the City Bowl and harbour below and forward inside the mountain group's amphitheatre. Treat the tablecloth as a summit/plateau cap rather than general sky cloud: <https://www.southafrica.net/uk/en/travel/article/explore-the-rugged-intensely-beautiful-mountain-ranges-of-south-africa>.

- **Rights conclusion:** Use the page for factual geometry citation only unless separate written permission is obtained for its imagery.

### 2. SANParks — viewer-relative Lion's Head placement

- **Exact source URL:** <https://www.sanparks.org/parks/table-mountain/what-to-do/attractions/lions-head-signal-hill>
- **Source/owner:** South African National Parks (SANParks); no individual photographer credit is stated in the accessible page text.
- **Page accessed / factual use:** Publicly reachable. SANParks states that Lion's Head is to the right of Table Mountain when the mountain is faced head-on, and that Lion's Head has views of both the city and Table Mountain: <https://www.sanparks.org/parks/table-mountain/what-to-do/attractions/lions-head-signal-hill>.
- **Geometry-only facts supported:** In the conventional frontal city-side composition, keep Lion's Head on frame-right of the broad Table Mountain block. This is viewpoint-dependent, so preserve the “facing it head on” qualifier: <https://www.sanparks.org/parks/table-mountain/what-to-do/attractions/lions-head-signal-hill>.
- **Stated image/content usage terms:** SANParks states that its articles, photographs, graphics, and images may not be reproduced, adapted, distributed, stored, modified, transmitted, or recorded without prior written consent; it also states that use of website photographs without the necessary permit is unlawful: <https://www.sanparks.org/account/register.php>.
- **Rights conclusion:** The official page is a factual orientation source, not an open-image source.
- **Restricted photo retained:** **No.**

### 3. City of Cape Town — east/west orientation and city-at-foot relationship

- **Exact source URL:** <https://resource.capetown.gov.za/documentcentre/Documents/Procedures,%20guidelines%20and%20regulations/Company's%20Garden%20Self%20Guided%20Walk.pdf>
- **Source/owner:** City of Cape Town, *Company's Garden Self Guided Walk*, 2025 edition. The guide says that, unless otherwise indicated, its photographs, maps, illustrations, and other visual aids are City of Cape Town property: <https://resource.capetown.gov.za/documentcentre/Documents/Procedures,%20guidelines%20and%20regulations/Company's%20Garden%20Self%20Guided%20Walk.pdf>.
- **Page accessed / factual use:** The official guide was reachable as a PDF. Its orientation text states that Table Valley sits at Table Mountain's foot, flanked by Devil's Peak to the east and Lion's Head to the west: <https://resource.capetown.gov.za/documentcentre/Documents/Procedures,%20guidelines%20and%20regulations/Company's%20Garden%20Self%20Guided%20Walk.pdf>.
- **Geometry-only facts supported:** Anchor the city/valley below the mountain; on a north-to-south frontal view, east/Devil's Peak reads to the viewer's left and west/Lion's Head to the viewer's right. The first clause is stated by the City; the screen-side mapping follows from facing south and should not be carried to other viewpoints without rechecking the camera direction: <https://resource.capetown.gov.za/documentcentre/Documents/Procedures,%20guidelines%20and%20regulations/Company's%20Garden%20Self%20Guided%20Walk.pdf>.
- **Stated image/content usage terms:** The guide states City ownership of its visual aids unless otherwise indicated, but states no open reuse license on the guide page: <https://resource.capetown.gov.za/documentcentre/Documents/Procedures,%20guidelines%20and%20regulations/Company's%20Garden%20Self%20Guided%20Walk.pdf>.
- **Rights conclusion:** Treat the guide's visual material as permission-required; cite its text for orientation.
- **Restricted photo retained:** **No.**

### 5. NASA Earth Observatory — terrain and city context

- **Exact source URL:** <https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/cape-town-south-africa-4665/>
- **Source/credit:** NASA Earth Observatory; the page credits the image to the “SRTM Team NASA/JPL/NIMA”: <https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/cape-town-south-africa-4665/>.
- **Page accessed / factual use:** Publicly reachable. The visual is a perspective rendering made from Landsat imagery and Shuttle Radar Topography Mission elevation data; the page locates Cape Town's city centre at Table Bay adjacent to Table Mountain: <https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/cape-town-south-africa-4665/>.
- **Geometry-only facts supported:** Useful for the large-scale relationship of the city, Table Bay, Table Mountain, Cape Peninsula, and surrounding relief. It is not a literal camera view: NASA states that the terrain has **2× vertical exaggeration**, the back data edges form a false horizon, a false sky was added, and colours were enhanced: <https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/cape-town-south-africa-4665/>.
- **Stated image/content usage terms:** NASA says its content is generally not subject to U.S. copyright and may be used for educational or informational purposes; NASA must be acknowledged, use must not imply endorsement, and separately marked third-party copyrighted material requires permission from its holder: <https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/>.
- **Rights conclusion:** This is NASA-guideline material, not a Creative Commons photo. Preserve the page's “SRTM Team NASA/JPL/NIMA” credit and the rendering caveats if it is ever reused.
- **Restricted photo retained:** **No.** The page's downloadable JPEG was not downloaded.
