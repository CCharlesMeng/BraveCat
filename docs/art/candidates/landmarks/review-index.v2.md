## Known review cautions

- Forbidden City roof details are watercolor-simplified; review the compact corner footprint and interlocked multi-eave silhouette rather than expecting architectural line-drawing detail.
- Eiffel Tower, the Bund, and Brooklyn Bridge slot edges carry some quiet background detail; their real-Portrait separation passed final composite QA.
- Huawei source photos are editorial-use-only and were used for geometry. Commercial-rights and derivative-risk review remains required before shipping.
- CC/rights review remains required for retained or consulted geometry and companion-anatomy references, including attribution, adaptation, and share-alike applicability where relevant.
- Public-monument and artwork rights review remains required where applicable, including Christ the Redeemer and relevant freedom-of-panorama considerations.
- The Istanbul street-kitten Companion is explicitly non-player, directly painted into that companion-rare scene only, and is not a Portrait, standalone cat asset, pose set, or reusable animal asset.
