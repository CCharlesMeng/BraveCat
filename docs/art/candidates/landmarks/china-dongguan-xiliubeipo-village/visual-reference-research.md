# Visual references: Huawei Songshan Lake Xiliubeipo Village

Checked 2026-07-20. The first-party Huawei material is sufficient; no third-party photos are needed.

## Identity and naming

Huawei uses several labels for this site. Its English campus feature calls it **“OX HORN CAMPUS”** and places it on the southern shore of Dongguan's Songshan Lake; the same feature says the 300-acre campus has 12 European-city-inspired villages: <https://digitalpower.huawei.com/resource/public/campus/en/songshanlake.html>. Huawei's galleries use **“Huawei Xiliubeipo Village”** and **“松山湖华为溪流背坡村.”** A Huawei-authored article also calls the Songshan Lake site **“华为溪村‘欧洲小镇’”** and confirms 12 groups, individually styled façades, and pitched roofs: <https://www.huawei.com/cn/huaweitech/publication/202302/urban-digital-twin-smart-cities>.

Huawei does not use one English label consistently, so retain each source's own wording in attribution. Do not substitute generic “Songshan Lake” factory photographs: Huawei's media library also contains unrelated manufacturing-site images.
