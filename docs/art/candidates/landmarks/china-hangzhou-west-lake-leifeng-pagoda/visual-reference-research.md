## Geometry findings used for generation

- The present Leifeng Pagoda is a five-storey, eight-sided tower on Xizhao (Sunset) Hill on the south side of West Lake.
- Its long-view recognition points are five clearly separated stacked roof tiers, wide upturned eaves that narrow upward, a compact dark central body, and a tall pointed finial.
- From the Long Bridge / lake-side relationship, the pagoda sits left of center on a densely wooded hill rather than at the waterline. Broad West Lake water occupies the foreground, and lower, layered hill ridges continue behind and to the right.
- The classic “Leifeng Pagoda in Evening Glow” view depends on the pagoda remaining a crisp silhouette above the tree canopy while water and distant hills stay quieter.
- A restrained causeway or shore edge may be suggested, but boats, people, signs, buildings, and other incidental photographic details are excluded.
