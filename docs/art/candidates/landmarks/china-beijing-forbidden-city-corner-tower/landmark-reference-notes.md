# 故宫角楼 v02 geometry reference

## Geometry findings

- The Palace Museum describes the corner tower as a compact pavilion on a Xumi plinth at the junction of the city walls, surrounded by a stone balustrade and integrated with the moat-side defensive enclosure.
- Its plan is cross-shaped: a central three-bay square pavilion has a projecting porch on each face.
- The silhouette is not a stack of repeated pagoda tiers. The upper roof is an intersecting hip-and-gable form with four visible gables and a central gilded finial; four middle projecting hip-and-gable porch roofs and linked lower half-slope eaves create a dense, interlocked ridge network.
- The v02 acceptance silhouette therefore requires a compact wall-corner footprint, multiple lateral roof projections, irregular-but-coherent upturned eave points, and a low wall/moat relationship.
