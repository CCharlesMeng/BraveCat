# Singapore Merlion enrichment v4 — social context research

Research date: 2026-07-20  
Method: official-source text review only. No source photographs, screenshots, or other restricted media were downloaded or retained.

## Findings

### Merlion Park and water activity

Singapore Tourism Board’s official Merlion Park page places the 8.6-metre Merlion in front of the Fullerton Hotel, overlooking Marina Bay. It identifies the lion head, fish body, and mouth water jet as defining features. The same page describes strolling along the Singapore River and taking a bumboat ride. This supports a pedestrian waterfront with small incidental visitors and one generic, unmarked low-profile harbour/river boat, while the statue profile and water jet remain the scene’s dominant landmarks.

### Promenade, shade, and night character

URA describes the Marina Bay waterfront as a continuous 3.5-kilometre pedestrian loop. Its planning material calls for a well-shaded pedestrian environment, cooling water features, solar-powered fans, shops and food-and-beverage uses, integrated LED lighting, and external building lighting that makes the skyline appreciable at night. This supports sparse walkers, a broad unobstructed walking route, credible warm promenade fixtures, and restrained blue-hour skyline reflections. It does not support a neon-heavy nightlife treatment.

URA also documents Breeze Shelters as shaded pavilions and pause points with large fans. Those shelters are useful contextual evidence for a cool, dry rest area elsewhere on the continuous waterfront, but the rest scene should not falsely present a Breeze Shelter as immediately beside the Merlion.

### Covered dining/rest context

The Fullerton Heritage describes One Fullerton as a two-storey waterfront dining and entertainment development directly adjacent to Merlion Park, with Marina Bay views, an urban promenade, and all-day use by tourists, workers, and residents. This makes an unbranded covered waterfront arcade or quiet café/window-seat interpretation credible near the Merlion. A low-detail cushioned bench may be reserved as the sleep composition slot; the scene should not imply a named venue, unrestricted overnight public sleeping, or access to a private area.

### Social density

The official descriptions frame the area as a public promenade, visitor landmark, dining area, and urban gathering point. Daytime should therefore include a modest number of small visitors/walkers, while blue hour and the sheltered interior should remain active but sparse. Faces must be non-identifiable. All people and the boat must remain outside the Portrait safeBounds and secondary to the Merlion, water jet, bay, and skyline.

## Rights and usage caution

The STB Brand Assets page states that the Merlion Symbol, and representations resembling it, receive statutory protection under Section 39 of the Singapore Tourism Board Act 1963. STB accepts applications for permission, bars use as a trademark or logo or in a way that suggests applicant ownership/endorsement, and says users remain responsible for other permissions and licences, including copyright. Some tourism/souvenir and non-commercial-photo uses may not require an application, but a stylised Merlion scene inside a commercial game is not safely assumed to fall within those examples.

Accordingly, these generated scenes remain non-shipping candidates. Before promotion or commercial release, obtain a specific legal/rights determination and, if required, written STB permission for the stylised Merlion depiction. Do not use the Merlion as the game’s logo, app icon, trademark, or implied STB/Singapore endorsement. The official webpages were used for factual text research only; their photographs and page artwork were not used as generation references.
