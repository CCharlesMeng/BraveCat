## Production geometry synthesis

- Keep the statue body slightly taller than its hand-to-hand width: approximately 30:28 before adding the pedestal.
- Keep exactly two nearly horizontal arms, one continuous limb per shoulder, with one hand at each endpoint; preserve one head, one robe mass, and one pedestal.
- Keep the head unusually small against the long body and use broad, restrained Art Deco robe folds rather than naturalistic loose fabric.
- Separate the pale monument from a low, complete pedestal and place both on the narrow crown of a steep, forested Corcovado mass.
- Simplify Rio into layered ridges descending to pale bay water, with a small distant Sugarloaf-like cone as context rather than a competing focal point.
- These relationships are factual geometry. No restricted photograph was stored, transformed, traced, or used as an image-generation reference.
