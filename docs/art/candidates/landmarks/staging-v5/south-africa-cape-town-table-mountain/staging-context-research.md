## Official text findings

### Summit access and circulation

- SANParks and the Table Mountain Aerial Cableway announced a Universal Access Walkway at the Table Mountain summit in April 2026 for visitors using wheelchairs or prams and for people with mobility challenges. This supports a stable, intentionally accessible summit circulation surface.
- The Cableway's official wheelchair guide says a large part of the looped pathway at the Upper Station is wheelchair accessible, while identifying stairs separately. It also marks viewpoints and a view terrace. This supports an overlook on broad, maintained ground rather than a precarious natural cliff lip.
- SANParks tells visitors to remain on demarcated paths, avoid shortcuts and ravines, monitor rapidly changing weather, and use the mountain in groups. These instructions require the Scene Perch to sit safely within established-looking plateau circulation and visibly back from any drop.

Scene implications:

- Frame the plateau, layered Cape landscape, and distant relief as the subject. Use a broad, stable lookout or loop-path surface with a clear setback from the edge and a believable route into the scene.
- Reserve a tiny distant `gaze` Perch at target scale `0.07`. The Cat appears only in the QA preview; the scene candidate remains Cat-free.
- Keep the complete preview silhouette clear of rails, rocks, people, posts, stairs, hard shadows, and foreground occlusion. Maintain natural local contrast without a spotlight, outline, empty box, or artificial clearing.
- Any incidental visitors must be sparse and asymmetrical on the maintained route, with varied depth, direction, lateral position, and gesture; no edge lineup, equal spacing, clones, synchronized poses, or identifiable faces.

### Access qualification

- The official material establishes accessible summit routes, not universal access to every plateau surface. The artwork therefore depicts a plausible maintained lookout area near the summit loop, without claiming that an exact pictured rock or coordinate is open to visitors.
- Table Mountain is a serious mountain environment. The scene must not normalize standing on an unprotected cliff edge, leaving marked paths, or using a closed or ecologically sensitive area.

### Rights and permits

- SANParks states that commercial film and stills shoots in Table Mountain National Park require an application and permit, are assessed for impact, and are subject to dates and location-specific restrictions.
- SANParks' website terms state that its articles, photographs, drawings, graphics, logos, images, films, text, and data may not be reproduced, adapted, distributed, stored, modified, transmitted, or recorded without prior written consent.
- These official sources were consulted for text facts only. No website photograph, screenshot, map, PDF image, logo, or other source media was downloaded or retained, and no source pixels are used in the candidate.
- A physical-location filming permit is not automatically the legal test for a generated illustration, but it is a material site-management warning. Final source-independence, national-park/landmark depiction, commercial-use, and artwork-rights review remain required before shipping.
