## Current official verification

1. **Beijing Mutianyu Great Wall Scenic Area, “Mutianyu Great Wall tour routes”**  
   https://en.mutianyugreatwall.com/article/4GUFeYKhPVz
   - Page date: 2024-02-07; checked 2026-07-20.
   - The official west route takes visitors by cable car to Watchtower No. 14 and then on foot toward Watchtower No. 20; the site describes most of that route as relatively flat, with the No. 19–20 segment the steepest.
   - The official east route takes visitors by ropeway to Watchtower No. 6 and then on foot toward Watchtower No. 1.
   - Art consequence: ordinary visitors belong on the established inner walking route and viewing areas, never on crenellation caps, tower roofs, cliff lips, vegetation slopes, or unsupported masonry.

2. **Beijing Mutianyu Great Wall Scenic Area, “Head of State Reception Route”**  
   https://en.mutianyugreatwall.com/article/4MCmIYTEC1j
   - Page date shown at retrieval: 2026-07-08; checked 2026-07-20.
   - The official route enters through the North Ticket Gate, uses the cable car to the No. 14 Watchtower Viewing Platform, visits No. 15, and returns to the No. 14 platform.
   - The site characterizes this as a lighter-workout route.
   - Perch consequence: a generalized broad inner viewing-platform paving connected to the sanctioned walking route is access-plausible. The source establishes platform access, not the exact geometry or emptiness of the generated lookout.

3. **People’s Government of Beijing Municipality, “Mutianyu Great Wall”**  
   https://english.beijing.gov.cn/travellinginbeijing/attractions/202603/t20260325_4566115.html
   - Page date: 2026-03-25; checked 2026-07-20 through official indexed text after direct retrieval timed out.
