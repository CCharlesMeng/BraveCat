# 上海外滩 staging-v5 context research

Research date: 2026-07-20  
Destination: `china-shanghai-the-bund` (上海外滩 / the Bund)  
Use: factual circulation, access, lighting, vessel, and rights planning only

## Staging conclusions

- The regulated Bund scenic area is a public riverfront zone on the west bank of the Huangpu. The official boundary runs from the west-bank river edge to the west sidewalks of East Zhongshan No. 1 and No. 2 Roads. The promenade is therefore a credible public walking Perch; the motor road remains a separate surface.
- The municipal waterfront design describes three continuous north-south pedestrian routes formed by the floodwall-top route, ramps, platforms, plazas, and ground-level paths. It also separates the reduced surface motor road from the riverfront public realm. Scene people belong on the promenade; generic cars belong only on the road.
- The management rule protects the floodwall, river-edge barriers, lamps, and other public facilities. A proposed Portrait must stand on open paving, not on the wall, rail, chain, lamp base, planting, or traffic surface.
- Official 2025 transport reporting confirms active Shanghai Ferry operations and trial preparation for new-energy vessels on the Dongjin line. Small generic ferries at unequal depth are plausible on the river, but route marks, operator livery, vessel text, and exact protected designs are omitted.
- Ordinary evening staging uses a legible promenade and restrained static lighting. It does not imitate a festival mode, media facade, laser show, or branded night-tour presentation.

## Variant application

### Day-signature v03

- Foreground Perch: broad dry promenade paving, anchor `(0.130, 0.940)`, `walk`, scale `0.210`.
- Ambient Life: one offset loose pair plus three isolated walkers, with decreasing apparent scale, mixed direction and gesture, unequal gaps, and varied lateral lanes.
- Three generic cars remain on the separate road at irregular intervals. Two unmarked ferries occupy the river at markedly different distances.
- The first generated attempt was rejected because three near walkers read as one same-size plane. Its normalized artifact is retained for audit; v03 uses the replacement attempt.

### Night-transit v02

- Distant Perch: readable pale promenade paving, anchor `(0.470, 0.670)`, `walk`, scale `0.080`, horizontally flipped to face into the route.
- One loose group of three and isolated walkers occupy different depths and lateral locations. The Perch remains between, rather than inside, their circulation lanes.
- Generic road vehicles and two river craft use visibly irregular spacing. Warm lamps and static facade light preserve the ground plane without neon dominance.

## Cultural and social cautions

- Use neutral place language: 上海外滩, the Bund, 黄浦江, and 浦东陆家嘴.
- Do not use qipao staging, rickshaws, decorative lantern clichés, pseudo-Chinese writing, flags, police, demonstrations, or romanticized colonial-era slogans as shorthand for contemporary Shanghai.
- People are incidental public-space users. Faces remain non-identifiable; clothing, vehicles, and vessels carry no readable marks.
- A public promenade does not imply endorsement by Shanghai authorities, transport operators, building owners, or tenants.
