Method: official-source text review only. Official photographs, screenshots, page artwork, and other remote media were not downloaded, retained, or supplied to image generation.

## Place and circulation findings

- Singapore Tourism Board’s Merlion Park page identifies the 8.6-metre, 70-tonne lion-head/fish-body statue, its mouth water jet, its position in front of the Fullerton Hotel, and its outlook over Marina Bay. Those are the required recognition relationships.
- The same page describes strolling along the Singapore River and bumboat travel. This supports ordinary pedestrian circulation and small generic boat movement, but not marked tour craft, readable route details, or a staged crowd.
- URA’s Marina Bay planning material describes a continuous pedestrian waterfront loop and a walkable, shaded public realm. This supports a broad dry promenade Perch and sparse walkers at different depths. It does not support putting the Portrait in the bay, jet splash, a railing, or an obstructed edge.

## Merlion legal caveat

STB’s Brand Assets page, last updated 2026-03-10, says STB administers statutory protection of the Merlion Symbol under Section 39 of the Singapore Tourism Board Act 1963. It invites applications for the Merlion Symbol and resembling representations, including artwork. The page lists limited no-application examples such as specified souvenirs, tourism packaging/collateral, and non-commercial photography or film. A stylised depiction in a commercial game is not safely assumed to fit those examples.

STB also states that permission may be denied for trademark/logo use or use that shifts association from STB or Singapore to the applicant. Conditions prohibit unapproved blocking or superimposition and perceived commercial endorsement, and leave users responsible for other approvals such as copyright.

Accordingly:

- keep both candidates non-shipping;
- obtain a specific legal determination and, if required, written STB permission before promotion or commercial release;
- do not use the depiction or the word “Merlion” as a game logo, app icon, trademark, or implied STB/Singapore endorsement;
- do not infer that generation, internal review, or candidate approval clears statutory, trademark, copyright, or site-policy rights.
