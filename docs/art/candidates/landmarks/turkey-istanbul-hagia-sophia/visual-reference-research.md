# Visual reference research: Istanbul Hagia Sophia

Checked 2026-07-20. Sources were consulted for factual exterior geometry only. No source photograph was downloaded, copied into the repository, supplied to the image generator, or retained locally.

## Geometry findings used

- Hagia Sophia reads as a broad, irregular, heavily buttressed Byzantine basilica mass, not as a conventionally symmetrical Ottoman mosque.
- One very large, shallow central dome dominates the silhouette. A close ring of arched windows marks its base.
- Large east and west half-domes, followed by smaller semi-domed exedrae and lower roof masses, create the characteristic stepped cascade.
- Massive pier and buttress forms visibly brace the north and south sides.
- Four Ottoman minarets occupy the four corners of the building ensemble. Their differing dates explain subtle differences in shaft material and proportions; perspective may stagger them, but the four-corner arrangement must remain coherent.
