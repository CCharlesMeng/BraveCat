## Approved Chinese specification

### 故事目标

Minho 在一块尚未留下痕迹的浅雪地上，走出一个首尾相接的小圆圈。这是安全、
自主、非任务式的目标：它不追逐移动目标，不获得纪念物，也不受环境人物指挥。

### 第一幕：未落脚的雪（establish）

雪刚停。Minho 从干燥回廊中 `gaze` 向庭院中央一块完整的椭圆形浅雪地；日光正从
远端慢慢靠近，目标明确但尚未开始。

- Perch：回廊内干燥石面。
- Cat Framing：中远景。
- Ambient Life：远处维护者清扫另一条通道，不与 Minho 互动。
- Postcard：`那一小块雪还没有脚印，我想去走一圈。`

### 第二幕：放弃发亮的近路（transit and choice）

通往雪地的直线路面结了薄冰。Minho 没有冒险穿过，而是转入与第一幕门廊连续的
干燥石质回廊 `walk`，沿庭院边缘绕行，再从低矮石门槛进入雪地。路线更长但安全，
且不凭空出现临时木板结构。

- Perch：有屋檐遮蔽、表面粗糙防滑的宽石板回廊。
- Cat Framing：中景，Minho 占画面高度约 16–18%，冰面、连续回廊和雪地入口同时可见。
- Ambient Life：一名访客沿独立出口离开。
- Postcard：`近路亮得太滑，我从木廊慢慢绕过去。`

### 第三幕：圆圈接上了（fulfillment）

Minho 在只有爪深的松软浅雪上 `walk`，新鲜脚印沿椭圆边缘逐渐闭合；最后一步接近
第一枚脚印，愿望通过自己的移动明确完成。

- Perch：下方为平整土地的浅雪层，无坑洞或高差。
- Cat Framing：猫高度的侧向中远景，不使用俯视图。
- Ambient Life：远处维护者推着普通空车经过外侧通道。
- Postcard：`最后一枚脚印，刚好接上了第一枚。`

### 第四幕：把圆圈留给太阳（quiet aftermath）

Minho 回到相连的干燥矮石阶上 `sit`。阳光开始软化外圈脚印，但完整轮廓仍清晰
可见；Minho 没有带走任何东西，只安静看着自己的痕迹逐渐回到庭院。

- Perch：屋檐下宽而干燥的低石阶。
- Cat Framing：中远景，让 Minho、圆形脚印和来路共同收束。
- Ambient Life：维护者已经远离，庭院恢复安静。
- Postcard：`太阳把边缘变软了，中间那一圈还在。`

### Album closing line

`它没有带走什么，只把一段白雪走成自己的圆，又坐着看太阳慢慢把它交还给庭院。`

### Continuity locks

- Every panel contains exactly one Minho and exactly one Pack.
- Minho remains the same compact, sturdy silver-shaded cat with blue-green eyes,
  a thick tapered tail with soft ring markings, and no body accessory other than
  the travel Pack.
- The Pack remains worn on Minho's back in all four panels: one small olive-green
  canvas Pack, one tan flap, two tan buckle straps, and one rolled cream blanket
  attached on top. It is never removed, duplicated, enlarged, recolored, or
  redesigned.
- Stable location anchors: one oval shallow-snow bed, low reddish-brown masonry,
  a continuous curved covered stone arcade, simple stone drains, a low dry stone
  threshold into the snow, a dry low step, and a few slender bare trunks.
- Walkable route: dry lower arcade -> continuous curved covered stone walkway ->
  low dry threshold at the snow-bed edge -> shallow snow loop -> connected dry
  low step under the opposite eave.
- Time/weather progression: snow has just stopped under cool cloud -> cloud gap
  widens -> clear noon sunlight at fulfillment -> gentle early-afternoon thaw.
- All views remain near cat height. Every paw, Pack, board, step, and background
  figure has credible scale, support, contact, and shadow.

### Plot-collision audit

Closest known prior story: `reed-mist-morning-flight`, summarized by the user as
following a bird and receiving a feather.

- Prior noun-free skeleton: notice a moving target -> follow it through a route
  change -> reach it -> retain a remnant associated with it.
- New noun-free skeleton: choose a blank surface -> reject an unsafe shortcut ->
  create a self-chosen pattern through movement -> observe the temporary trace
  soften without taking anything away.
- Material differences: acquisition becomes creation; target-following becomes
  a safety judgment; receiving or contacting an external target becomes drawing
  a path with Minho's own movement; possessing a keepsake becomes accepting an
  ephemeral trace.
